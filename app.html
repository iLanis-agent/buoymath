Buoy Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out density, displacement and spare lift from the numbers you type. The mass, volume and fluid density are yours to measure or read off your own setup. It treats every body as uniform and every fluid as still, so it has not seen your setup and does not know the body's shape, balance, leaks or condition, the water's movement, or how anything is loaded or rigged, and it gives no verdict that anything is safe to float, ride, tow or load. Every number is your own entry.

The density truth

A body's mass and volume set its density against the fluid's. Mass in kg, volume in L, fluid density in kg/m³.

The draft truth

How much of the body's volume sits under the surface when it floats freely, using the entries above.

The lift truth

What the fluid buoys at full submersion, and the spare lift before the body sits awash, using the entries above.

Buoy Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
